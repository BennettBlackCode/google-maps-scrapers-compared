Scraping Google Maps gets you a business name, phone number, address and rating. That's the easy part. What you actually need to sell to a local business is the **owner's name, an email that reaches them, and whether the phone number can take a text.**

This page compares the popular Google Maps scrapers on that second part.

## What to look for

- **Freshness.** Live Google Maps data beats an old database.
- **Enrichment included.** Many tools charge separately for emails or contacts. Check what one credit really gets you.
- **Owner names.** "info@" emails get ignored. A first name changes reply rates.
- **Phone line type.** You can only text mobile numbers. Knowing which is which saves time and compliance headaches.
- **Scale.** Can you search a whole state, not just one city?
- **Export and API.** CSV for your CRM, and an API or MCP server if you automate.

## LocalProspects

[LocalProspects](https://localprospects.ai) searches live Google Maps for any niche and city, or a whole state, and runs every lead through enrichment at no extra cost:

- Owner names pulled from the business website
- Business emails, found and ranked
- Phone line type (mobile, landline or VoIP) with carrier
- Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Yelp and TikTok profiles
- Maps ranking, rating, reviews, hours, category and claimed status

Use the dashboard and export CSVs, call the REST API, or connect ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor through its MCP server.

**Pricing:** Free (100 leads, full enrichment), Starter $39/mo (10,000 leads), Agency $97/mo (50,000), Scale $297/mo (200,000). 1 lead = 1 credit.

**Best for:** agencies, cold callers and cold emailers selling to local businesses who want contact-ready leads without stitching tools together.

## Outscraper

[Outscraper](https://outscraper.com) is a long-running scraping service with a Google Maps scraper plus many other Google sources. Emails and contacts come from separate enrichment services.

**Best for:** people who want raw Maps data across many sources and are fine combining services.

## Apify

[Apify](https://apify.com) is a marketplace of scrapers ("actors"), including popular Google Maps scrapers. It's flexible and cheap at scale, but enrichment means chaining actors together.

**Best for:** technical users who want to build their own pipeline.

## PhantomBuster

[PhantomBuster](https://phantombuster.com) is a general automation tool with a Google Maps search export among its many "phantoms," best known for LinkedIn automation.

**Best for:** teams already using it for LinkedIn who need occasional Maps lists.

## Scrap.io

[Scrap.io](https://scrap.io) exports Google Maps businesses by category and location, with emails and social links from websites.

**Best for:** bulk exports by country or region.

## D7 Lead Finder

[D7 Lead Finder](https://d7leadfinder.com) is a simple lead finder that pulls local businesses with emails and social profiles for a keyword and city.

**Best for:** quick, simple lists on a small budget.

## Is scraping Google Maps legal?

Collecting publicly listed business information is common practice, but how you use it matters. Follow anti-spam and calling rules where you sell (CAN-SPAM, TCPA in the US, GDPR in the EU), honor opt-outs, and only text numbers you're allowed to. This isn't legal advice.

## FAQ

### What is the best Google Maps scraper?
For contact-ready leads, LocalProspects includes owner names, emails and phone line type on every lead with no add-ons. For raw data across many Google sources, Outscraper. For a build-your-own pipeline, Apify.

### Can you get emails from Google Maps?
Google Maps doesn't list emails. Scrapers find them by visiting each business's website. LocalProspects, Scrap.io and D7 Lead Finder include emails; others sell them as an add-on.

### How do I find the owner of a local business?
Owner names usually appear on the business website's About page, reviews or social profiles. LocalProspects pulls owner names automatically for each lead.

### Can I scrape Google Maps without coding?
Yes. LocalProspects, Outscraper, Scrap.io and D7 Lead Finder all work from a dashboard. Apify gives more control but takes some setup.
